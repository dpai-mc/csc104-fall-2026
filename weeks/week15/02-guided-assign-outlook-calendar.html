Calendar ideas in this module

The training's tasks include using multiple calendars, switching views, appointments, reminders, recurring appointments and meetings (2:28). A few words it uses:

Outlook calendar terms, with the moment in the video where each one comes up.
TermWhat it meansIn the video
AppointmentSomething on your own calendar, like "Workshop Prep" at 1 PM. It has a subject, a time and, optionally, a location.
MeetingAn appointment you invite other people to.
Show AsThe status other people see when they look at your calendar: Busy, Tentative, Working Elsewhere and so on.
Recurring appointmentOne that repeats on a schedule, such as every Monday.
ReminderAn alert before an appointment starts.
Calendar viewsDay, week or month layouts, and more than one calendar shown side by side.

Windows and Mac differences

The simulation runs in your browser, so it behaves the same on both. Pick your platform.

On Windows

  • The simulated Outlook is Windows Outlook. Appointment options such as Show As are on the Appointment tab of the ribbon.
  • Pop-ups: if the training window doesn't open, allow pop-ups for MindTap in your browser.

On Mac

The simulation shows Windows Outlook even on a Mac, so click what it shows you. If something genuinely won't work, message me through the Canvas Inbox.

  • Mac Help in the training explains how each task is done in real Outlook for Mac.
  • Right-click is Control-click, or a two-finger click on a trackpad.
  • Pop-ups: in Safari, allow pop-ups for MindTap if the training window doesn't open.
