Word Project: Community Newsletter

This is the end of the Word unit. You build a two-page newsletter, plus a cover page, from scratch, using what you've learned in Word Modules 1 through 4. There's no start file and no walkthrough this time. Every skill here is covered in the textbook and in the assignments you've already done, so if you don't remember how to do something, look it up.

Before you start

You're a volunteer coordinator for Lakewood Community Center. The Center wants a newsletter announcing this season's programs, how to register, and where the budget goes. You'll start from a new blank document in the desktop version of Word. The web and phone versions of Word don't have all of these commands.

Use only the text on this page

Each part that needs text has a Copy text button. Paste into Word with Paste › Keep Text Only (Mac: Edit › Paste and Match Formatting) so you don't bring this web page's formatting along. Don't add, reword or generate any content of your own. I grade the formatting, and that only works if everyone has the same words.

Mac users

Most of this works the same on a Mac. Where the Mac version doesn't have the exact option named, a blue note under that task tells you what to use instead. If you're still stuck, message me through Canvas Inbox.

About AI on this project

The course AI policy is limited. You can ask an AI tool to explain a command, tell you where something is on the Mac ribbon, or help you figure out why a step isn't working. You can't have it build or edit the file you submit. You make every change in Word yourself. Spelling and grammar check are ordinary Word features and always fine.

Work in this order

The order matters in two places: set up the Normal style before you paste any text, and insert the cover page last, so it lands in front of everything you've built.

  1. Document setup · 15 pts
  2. Page 1 text and formatting · 40 pts
  3. The two lists · 30 pts
  4. Picture and page break · 30 pts
  5. Budget table · 40 pts
  6. SmartArt · 15 pts
  7. Header, watermark, cover page · 25 pts

Saving with the right file name is the last 5 points. Save early and often. A copy in OneDrive or on a USB drive protects you if your laptop dies the night before.

The project, part by part

Open a new blank document and do these three things before you paste any text.

  1. Theme colors: change the theme colors to Blue Warm. Change the colors only. Leave the theme itself alone. Every "Accent 1" color later in this project comes from this palette.

    Mac: if Blue Warm isn't in your Colors list, use Blue II. The color positions used later stay the same.

    5 pts

  2. Modify the Normal style: font size 11 pt and Spacing After 6 pt. Change the style itself, not just the text.

    5 pts

  3. Page border: a Box border with a single solid line, color Accent 1, width 1½ pt, applied to the whole document.

    5 pts

Paste the text below into your document as plain text. It's every paragraph for page 1. Then apply the formatting. Each task is graded on its own, so a miss in one doesn't cost you points in another. Leave the two lists alone for now. They're Part 3.

Page 1 text

Welcome to Our New Season!
We are thrilled to announce another exciting season of programs and events at Lakewood Community Center. This newsletter covers everything you need to know about our upcoming offerings, how to register, and where our budget goes. Our programs are open to everyone: all ages and skill levels welcome.
Programs offered this season include:
Youth Soccer League — Saturdays, starting October 17
Adult Fitness Boot Camp — Monday and Wednesday evenings
Senior Art Workshop — Tuesday afternoons, all levels
Family Movie Nights — last Friday of each month, free admission
To register for any program, follow these steps:
Create your free online account
Go to the registration page on our website
Choose a username and password
Pick your programs
Pay your fees
Pay online by credit or debit card
Pay in person at the front desk
Watch for your confirmation email within two business days
Space is limited in every program, so we encourage all interested community members to register by November 13. Program schedules are subject to change without notice.

  1. Title line (Welcome to Our New Season!): 24 pt, Bold, centered, font color Accent 1, Darker 25%.

    6 pts

  2. Character spacing: expand the character spacing of the title line by 2 pt.

    4 pts

  3. Paragraph shading: shade the whole first body paragraph (it starts "We are thrilled to announce…") Accent 1, Lighter 80%. Use paragraph shading, not text highlight.

    6 pts

  4. Small caps: format the words Lakewood Community Center in that same paragraph as small caps.

    5 pts

  5. Underline the exact phrase register by November 13 in the last paragraph.

    4 pts

  6. Italicize the exact phrase all ages and skill levels welcome. Leave the period after it unformatted.

    4 pts

  7. Format Painter: make the line Programs offered this season include: Bold with font color Accent 1, Darker 25%. Then use the Format Painter to copy that formatting onto the line To register for any program, follow these steps:. The two lines should look identical.

    5 pts

  8. Footnote: insert a footnote at the end of the last sentence, right after "…subject to change without notice." The footnote text is:

    Registration fees are non-refundable. Contact the front desk for exceptions.

    6 pts

Both lists are already in your document from Part 2. Here you turn them into real Word lists.

  1. Bulleted list: make the four program lines (Youth Soccer League through Family Movie Nights) a bulleted list with the default round bullet.

    5 pts

  2. Bullet color: change the color of those bullets, the bullet characters only, to Accent 1, Darker 25%. The program text stays black.

    6 pts

  3. Sort: sort the four bulleted program paragraphs in ascending (A to Z) order. When you're done, Adult Fitness Boot Camp is first and Youth Soccer League is last.

    7 pts

  4. Multilevel list: make the eight registration lines (Create your free online account through Watch for your confirmation email…) a multilevel list using the 1., a., i. numbering format. Then demote the four second-level lines so the list looks like this:

    1. Create your free online account
      1. Go to the registration page on our website
      2. Choose a username and password
    2. Pick your programs
    3. Pay your fees
      1. Pay online by credit or debit card
      2. Pay in person at the front desk
    4. Watch for your confirmation email within two business days
    12 pts

Add one picture to page 1, then push the budget onto its own page.

  1. Insert a picture of a park or an outdoor activity. Click at the start of the line Programs offered this season include: and insert it there. The picture can be your own photo or one from Word's own stock or online images. It has to be appropriate for school.

    4 pts

  2. Size: make the picture exactly 2.5" wide, with the height changing in proportion. Lock aspect ratio stays checked.

    6 pts

  3. Picture style: apply Soft Edge Rectangle.

    6 pts

  4. Position and wrapping: Middle Right with Square Text Wrapping. Text should wrap down the left side of the picture.

    6 pts

  5. Alt text: write one sentence that describes your picture for someone who can't see it, ending with a period. For example: Children kicking a soccer ball on a grassy field. Don't check "Mark as decorative."

    4 pts

  6. Page break: click at the very end of the last paragraph, after the footnote number, and insert a manual page break . Don't press Enter until you reach the next page. The empty page 2 is where the budget goes.

    4 pts

On the new page, add a heading and a budget table. Do the tasks in the order listed.

Page 2 heading

Season Budget Overview

  1. Heading style: apply the built-in Heading 1 style to Season Budget Overview. Use only the style. Don't change its font, size or color by hand.

    4 pts

  2. Insert a table of 3 columns × 4 rows on the blank line under the heading, and type in the header row and the three data rows below. Type the amounts exactly as shown, dollar signs and commas included.

    Rows 1–4 (type these first)
    CategoryItem DescriptionAllocated Budget
    FacilitiesBuilding maintenance and repairs$4,200
    ProgramsInstructor fees and supplies$5,800
    EventsMarketing and community events$1,500
    6 pts

  3. Table style: apply Grid Table 5 Dark – Accent 1.

    Mac: if you can't find this exact one, pick the closest dark Accent 1 grid style and add a Word comment on the table saying which style you used.

    6 pts

  4. Sort the table by Allocated Budget, Type: Number, Descending, with "My list has a header row" selected. Programs ends up first and Events last.

    6 pts

  5. Total row: add a new last row, then merge its first two cells. Type Total Budget in the merged cell and make it Bold. The table now has 5 rows.

    6 pts

  6. Formula: in the last cell of the total row, insert the formula =SUM(ABOVE) with the number format $#,##0.00;($#,##0.00). It should show $11,500.00. Don't type that number yourself. The grader checks that a formula is in the cell.

    8 pts

  7. Cell alignment: select the three data rows (rows 2–4, not the header or the total row) and apply Align Center, which centers the text both horizontally and vertically in each cell.

    4 pts

Click on the blank line below the table and add a process diagram showing how a season comes together.

  1. Insert SmartArt: Basic Process, from the Process category. It needs exactly three shapes, in this order. Delete any extra shapes.

    1. Plan Programs
    2. Open Registration
    3. Host Events
    8 pts

  2. SmartArt style and colors: with the whole diagram selected, not one box, apply the SmartArt style Moderate Effect and change the colors to Colorful – Accent Colors, the first choice under Colorful.

    7 pts

These finishing touches apply to the whole document. Do the cover page last.

  1. Page-number header: a Plain Number 3 page number at the top of the page. Then click just in front of the number and type Lakewood Newsletter followed by a space,.

    7 pts

  2. Page number color: select the page number in the header and change its font color to Accent 1, Darker 25%.

    4 pts

  3. Watermark: add the built-in DRAFT 1 text watermark.

    Mac: if DRAFT 1 isn't offered, use a text watermark reading DRAFT, diagonal, semitransparent.

    6 pts

  4. Cover page (last): the Slice (Light) cover page. Fill in the two placeholders:

    • Title: Lakewood Community Center
    • Subtitle: Programs and Events Newsletter

    If the cover page has any other placeholders, you can leave them alone. Only the title and subtitle are graded.

    Mac: if Slice (Light) isn't listed, use Slice (Dark) or any built-in cover page that has a title and subtitle.

    8 pts

Last, run a spelling and grammar check. Ignore anything it flags in "Lakewood". Then look over the whole document before you save. You should have three pages: the cover, the newsletter page, and the budget page.
