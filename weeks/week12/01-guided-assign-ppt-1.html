New ideas this week

PowerPoint is built from slides, and every slide is built from boxes. The instruction file uses these words without explaining them, so here they are in one place.

The PowerPoint ideas this assignment uses, with the moment in the video where each one comes up.
IdeaWhat it meansIn the video
Normal viewThe everyday editing view: slide thumbnails down the left, the current slide in the middle, the Notes pane underneath.
Notes paneSpeaker notes under the slide. The presenter sees them; the audience doesn't.
PlaceholderA ready-made box on a slide for a title, text, or a picture. The text inside it can be selected, or the whole box can.
Theme font (Headings)The theme's built-in title font, listed at the top of the Font list with "(Headings)" after it.
List levelHow far a bullet is indented. First level is the main point, second level sits under it, third under that. Increase and Decrease List Level move a paragraph in and out.
Smart guidesDashed lines that appear while you drag something, showing when it lines up with another object or the middle of the slide.
Proportional resizeChanging the size without stretching the picture. Type the width and PowerPoint sets the height to match.
Slide layoutA predefined arrangement of placeholders, such as Title and Content. Changing the layout rearranges what's already on the slide.
Slide Sorter viewAll the slides as thumbnails, for checking the order and moving slides around.

Two ways to put a picture exactly where MindTap wants it

Steps 3 and 8 both ask you to place a picture "as shown in" a figure. The grader checks the position to a hundredth of an inch, which is hard to hit by dragging.

  • By eye, with smart guides. Drag the picture until the dashed lines in the figure appear on your screen, then let go (10:04). That's what the figure is showing you.
  • By the numbers. Right-click the picture › Size and Position › Position, and type the horizontal and vertical positions, measured from the top-left corner (24:57). If the Graded Summary Report says a picture is in the wrong place, it tells you the position it expected. Type those, save, and submit again.

Get it close by eye first, then let the report tell you whether it needs the numbers. That's what I do in the video, on purpose (27:04).

The assignment, step by step

Four setup items, then the ten graded steps, then submitting. Each one says what to change; the time button next to it shows you how. Always go by the instruction file MindTap gave you. The wording below is a summary, and MindTap grades against its own file.

Four details worth knowing

  • Copy the slide text from the instruction file. It's quicker than typing and it gets the wording exactly right. When you paste, keep the text only, so the instruction file's formatting doesn't come with it (6:27).
  • Select the whole box for step 1. Clicking inside the title only puts the cursor there. Click the box's border, where the pointer turns into four arrows, and the font and color change everything in it (4:12).
  • Width, not dragging, for size. Type the width in Picture Format › Width. The height follows on its own (8:58).
  • Slide numbers shift in step 7. The new slide is 6 until you drag it to the end; after that it's 7, and step 9's slide 6 is Benefits of Ice Skating.

The numbers below follow the order of the instruction file as I read it in the video. If your file groups or numbers something differently, go by your file.

Windows and Mac differences

The steps are the same on both. Most of this week is on the Home, Picture Format and View tabs, which look much the same on Mac and Windows. Pick your platform.

On Windows

  • Enable Editing. Downloaded files open in Protected View with a yellow bar across the top. Select Enable Editing before anything works.
  • Notes: View › Normal, then the Notes button on the status bar at the bottom of the window.
  • Paste text only: right-click › under Paste Options, the Keep Text Only icon (the A).
  • List levels: Home › Increase List Level and Decrease List Level, or Tab and Shift+Tab at the start of a line.
  • Smart guides: on by default. To check, right-click an empty part of the slide › Grid and Guides › Smart Guides.
  • Size and position: Picture Format › Width; right-click the picture › Size and Position for the exact position.
  • Layouts and new slides: Home › Layout and Home › New Slide (the arrow under it picks the layout).
  • Spelling: Review › Spelling, or F7.
  • Slide Sorter: View › Slide Sorter.

On Mac

Cengage's material is written for Windows. Most of this assignment matches, but check these before you decide something is broken. I've done these assignments on a Mac myself. If a command genuinely isn't where I say it is, message me through the Canvas Inbox rather than guessing at a substitute.

  • No Protected View. There's no Enable Editing bar.
  • Right-click is Control-click, or a two-finger click on a trackpad.
  • Notes: View › Normal, then Notes on the status bar or the View menu.
  • Paste text only: after pasting, select the small Paste Options button beside the text › Keep Text Only.
  • Smart guides: View › Guides › Smart Guides in the menu bar.
  • Exact position: right-click the picture › Format Picture, then the Size & Properties section of the pane, under Position.
  • Spelling and Slide Sorter: Review › Spelling and View › Slide Sorter, as on Windows.
