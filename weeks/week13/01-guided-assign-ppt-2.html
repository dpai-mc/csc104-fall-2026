New ideas this week

This module is about objects on a slide: shapes, pictures and SmartArt, and how they stack and line up. The instruction file uses these words without explaining them, so here they are in one place.

The PowerPoint ideas this assignment uses, with the moment in the video where each one comes up.
IdeaWhat it meansIn the video
HyperlinkText that opens a web page when it's clicked during a slide show. PowerPoint underlines it and changes its color.
SmartArtA ready-made diagram built from your text: lists, processes, cycles. Convert to SmartArt turns an existing list into one.
Layout vs. styleFor SmartArt, the layout is the shape of the diagram (Vertical Block List, Horizontal Bullet List). The style is its look (Moderate Effect, Inset). They're picked in different places.
Ruler and guidesThe ruler measures in inches from the center of the slide, so "2 inches left of center" is the 2 on the left side. The guides are dashed lines through the center.
Picture fillA picture used to fill a shape, so the picture takes the shape's outline. Different from inserting a picture on its own.
LayersEvery object sits in front of or behind the others. Send Backward moves one layer; Send to Back moves behind everything.
GroupJoins selected objects into one, so they move and resize together.
Header and footerDate, slide number and footer text that appear on every slide you apply them to.
TransitionThe effect as one slide replaces the next in a slide show. A small star by the thumbnail shows a slide has one.
Presenter ViewThe presenter's screen during a slide show: the current slide, the next one, and your notes. Not graded this week.

Placing a shape on the ruler

Steps 5 and 6 give positions like "the right edge two inches to the left of center and the bottom edge at zero." Here's how to read that.

  • Zero is the middle of the slide, on both rulers. The horizontal guide is at zero vertically; the vertical guide is at zero horizontally.
  • Watch the ruler while you drag. A faint marker on each ruler follows the shape's edges. Drag with the four-headed arrow until the right edge marker sits on the 2 to the left of center and the bottom edge sits on zero (12:41).
  • Line the second oval up with the first. Drag it straight down until the smart guide shows its edges match the oval above, then put its top edge on zero (14:22).

If the Graded Summary Report says a position is off, fix just that shape and submit again.

Four details worth knowing

  • The start file has two slides; the finished one has three. Step 4 adds a copy of slide 1 as slide 2, step 5 adds a blank slide 3, and step 10 deletes the old slide, which is slide 4 by then.
  • SmartArt layout and SmartArt style are different menus. Steps 3 and 4 each ask for one of each. If yours doesn't match the figure, check the layout first (7:06).
  • A picture fill goes in a shape; step 8's picture goes on the slide. For step 7 use Shape Fill › Picture; for step 8 click off the shapes and use Insert › Pictures (18:02).
  • Step 9 groups everything except the footer text. Shift-click the two ovals and the student picture, and leave the footer out.

Windows and Mac differences

The steps are the same on both. Most of this week is on the Home, Shape Format, Picture Format and Transitions tabs, which look much the same on Mac and Windows. Pick your platform.

On Windows

  • Enable Editing. Downloaded files open in Protected View with a yellow bar across the top. Select Enable Editing before anything works.
  • Hyperlink: right-click the selected text › Link, or Ctrl+K.
  • SmartArt: Home › Convert to SmartArt in the Paragraph group; layouts and styles on the SmartArt Design tab.
  • Ruler and guides: the Ruler and Guides check boxes on the View tab.
  • Duplicate: right-click a slide › Duplicate Slide; Ctrl+D duplicates a selected shape.
  • Outline, shadow, picture fill: Shape Format › Shape Outline, Shape Effects, Shape Fill.
  • Align, layers, group: Picture Format (or Shape Format) › Align, Send Backward › Send to Back, Group.
  • Footer: Insert › Header & Footer.
  • Slide Show: F5 from the start; Esc to stop.

On Mac

Cengage's material is written for Windows. Most of this assignment matches, but check these before you decide something is broken. I've done these assignments on a Mac myself. If a command genuinely isn't where I say it is, message me through the Canvas Inbox rather than guessing at a substitute.

  • No Protected View. There's no Enable Editing bar.
  • Right-click is Control-click, or a two-finger click on a trackpad.
  • Hyperlink: Insert › Link, or Command+K.
  • Ruler and guides: the Ruler check box on the View tab; guides are under View › Guides in the menu bar.
  • Duplicate a shape: Command+D.
  • Select several objects: Shift-click, the same as Windows.
  • Footer: Insert › Header & Footer.
  • SmartArt, shape formatting, transitions: on the same tabs as Windows.
