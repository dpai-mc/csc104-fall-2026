New ideas this week

This module is mostly about galleries: pick the object, find the right tab, pick the named option. The instruction file uses these words without explaining them, so here they are in one place.

The PowerPoint ideas this assignment uses, with the moment in the video where each one comes up.
IdeaWhat it meansIn the video
CorrectionsPicture adjustments for sharpness, brightness and contrast. "Sharpen: 25%" is one of the preset choices.
WordArtText with a preset decorative style: fill, outline, glow, shadow. Each style's name lists its parts, which is how the instruction file names it.
Picture styleA preset frame and shape for a picture, such as Metal Oval.
Color toneA picture's warmth, measured as a color temperature in kelvin (K). Lower numbers look cooler and bluer; higher numbers look warmer.
CropTrimming the edges of a picture. Drag a crop handle in, then click outside the picture to accept it.
Align Selected ObjectsAn option at the bottom of the Align menu. Checked, Align Top lines objects up with each other; otherwise PowerPoint aligns them to the edge of the slide.
Video styleA preset frame for a video on a slide, like a picture style.
Text effectsExtra effects for WordArt. Transform bends the text into a shape such as a wave; 3-D Rotation tilts it.

Reading a smart-guide figure

Figures 1 and 2 show where an object goes by showing the dashed smart guides you'll see when it's in the right place. Drag slowly; the guides appear only for a moment.

  • A straight line means two edges line up, like the bottom of the picture and the bottom of the text box in Figure 1 (8:11).
  • Double-headed arrows mean two gaps are equal, like the space on each side of the picture in Figure 1, or Hiking's space on each side in step 4 (8:28).
  • A plus sign of a vertical and a horizontal guide means centered on the slide, as in Figure 2 (15:03).

The assignment, step by step

Four details worth knowing

  • Select the object first. The WordArt, Picture Format and Video Format galleries only show up when the right thing is selected. If you can't find a gallery, click the object and look again (4:49).
  • Use the exact names. Several styles look alike. Hover over a gallery choice to see its name, and match it to the instruction file.
  • Check Align Selected Objects before Align Top in step 4, so the three pictures line up with each other and not with the top of the slide (11:22).
  • The start file looks different from the last two modules. That's expected (0:45).

The six graded steps

  1. 1

    Slide 1: sharpen the picture; Park Proposal as WordArt

    Sharpen the title slide's picture with Sharpen: 25%. Convert the text Park Proposal to WordArt with the Fill: White; Outline: Green, Accent color 1; Glow: Green, Accent color 1 style.

  2. 2

    Slide 2: insert and style the lake picture

    Insert the lake support picture. Change its height to 6" and let the width adjust, apply the Metal Oval picture style, and change its color tone to Temperature: 5300 K.

  3. 3

    Slide 2: crop the sky and place the picture

    Crop about 0.5" from the top of the picture. Then use smart guides to place it to the right of the text, as in Figure 1: bottoms lined up, equal space on each side.

  4. 4

    Slide 3: delete one, space one, align three

    On Callahan Lake Park Recreation, delete the shape with the text Picnicking. Move Hiking so it's evenly spaced between Fishing and Swimming. Then select all three and use Align Top so their tops are level with each other.

  5. 5

    Slide 7: insert and style the video

    On Preserve and Protect, use the content placeholder to insert the lake video file. Apply the beveled rectangle video style your instruction file names.

  6. 6

    Slide 8: Questions? as WordArt

    Insert WordArt with the text Questions? in the Pattern Fill: Green, Accent color 1, 50%; Hard Shadow: Green, Accent color 1 style (third column, fourth row). Apply the Wave: Up transform and the Off Axis 1: Right 3-D rotation, make it 9" wide, and center it on the slide as in Figure 2.

Windows and Mac differences

The steps are the same on both. Most of this week is on the Picture Format, Shape Format and Video Format tabs, which look much the same on Mac and Windows. Pick your platform.

On Windows

  • Enable Editing. Downloaded files open in Protected View with a yellow bar across the top. Select Enable Editing before anything works.
  • Picture adjustments: Picture Format › Corrections, Color, Picture Styles, Crop and Height.
  • WordArt: Shape Format › WordArt Styles and Text Effects; Insert › WordArt for new WordArt.
  • Align: Shape Format or Picture Format › Align; check Align Selected Objects at the bottom first.
  • Video: the Insert Video icon in the content placeholder; styles on the Video Format tab.
  • Gallery names: hover over any choice for its name in a tooltip.

On Mac

Cengage's material is written for Windows. Most of this assignment matches, but check these before you decide something is broken. I've done these assignments on a Mac myself. If a command genuinely isn't where I say it is, message me through the Canvas Inbox rather than guessing at a substitute.

  • No Protected View. There's no Enable Editing bar.
  • Picture adjustments and WordArt: on the same Picture Format and Shape Format tabs as Windows.
  • Align: Arrange › Align on the format tab; check Align Selected Objects first, as on Windows.
  • Video: the video icon in the content placeholder, or Insert › Video.
  • Gallery names: hover for a tooltip, the same as Windows. If a name is cut off, widen the PowerPoint window.
