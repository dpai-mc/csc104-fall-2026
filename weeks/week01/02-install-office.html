Install Microsoft Office on Your Computer — Free

Every Mendocino College student gets the full desktop version of Microsoft Office at no cost. The short version is ten steps; the video below walks through all of them. The whole video is written out on this page, so you can read it instead of watching if that suits you better.

  • Cost: $0
  • Video: 12 minutes
  • Your time: about 15 minutes
  • You need: your college email address
  • Do it: Week 1, before MindTap work starts

Before you start

  • You must have, or create, a Microsoft account using your college (.edu) email address. A personal Outlook, Hotmail or Gmail address will not get you the free copy.
  • You need a Windows or Mac computer you are allowed to install software on.
  • Set aside about fifteen minutes. Most of that is the download and install running on its own.

You will be downloading and installing the full desktop version of the Microsoft Office suite. These are different from the web and phone versions — they have more features, and this course asks you to use features the web and phone apps do not have.

The one wrong turn to avoid

On the Microsoft Education site, the big Get started button for students leads to a 50% off offer and asks you to create a brand-new Microsoft account. That path dead-ends — it never hands you off to the college login, and it looks like you are being asked to pay.

Use the Microsoft 365 for free link instead. That one sends you to the Mendocino College sign-in, which is what unlocks the free desktop apps. If you have already gone down the 50%-off path, just back out and start again from Step 2 — nothing is broken and you have not been charged.

No Windows or Mac computer?

If you are on a Chromebook, or on a machine you cannot install software on, send me a message through the Canvas Inbox this week. We will set you up with Splashtop so you can remote into a campus lab computer that already has Office installed. What does not work is discovering the problem in Week 3.

The short version: ten steps

This is the whole process in order. Each time stamp jumps the video below to the part that shows that step, so if one of them goes sideways you can watch just that piece.

  1. 1

    Open the Microsoft Office for Education site

    Type or paste this address into your browser:

    www.microsoft.com/en-us/education/products/office

  2. 2

    Choose Microsoft 365 for free — not the student offer

    Scroll past Get started for students. That button leads to the 50%-off page, which is the dead end described above. The free option is the one that mentions signing in with a school account.

    This is the step people get wrong

  3. 3

    Sign in with your college email address

    Choose Sign in and enter your full Mendocino College address. The page should hand you off to the college's own login screen — that hand-off is how you know you are on the right path. If you are already signed in to your college account in that browser, it may go straight through.

    If Microsoft says it cannot find an account, check that you used the complete college address, not a personal one.

  4. 4

    Ignore the Copilot page you land on

    Signing in drops you into Copilot. That is not where the installer lives, and you do not need Copilot for this class. Leave it alone and go to the next step.

  5. 5

    Follow the click path to the installer

    Apps and more →then All apps →then Install apps

    Install apps sits in the upper right corner of the All apps page. It is easy to miss, and it is the button that matters.

  6. 6

    Pick Microsoft 365 apps, then Install Office

    Choose Microsoft 365 apps — every student in this course has that, whatever the earlier pages implied. Then select Install Office and let the setup file download.

  7. 7

    Run the setup file and let it finish

    Open the downloaded file. Windows asks “Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?” — choose Yes. The install runs on its own; three to five minutes is normal. You will see “You're all set” when it is done.

    On a Mac

    You get a .dmg file instead. Open it, run the installer inside, and give your Mac password when it asks. There is no “allow changes” prompt and no restart prompt at the end.

  8. 8

    Open Word and sign in one more time

    Find Word in your apps list and open it. It will ask you to sign in again, with the same college address. This is normal and it only happens once — it is what links your installed copy to your college license. Expect a password and then a code sent to your phone.

    Stay signed in? is up to you. Either answer works.

  9. 9

    If it asks you to restart, restart Word — not your computer

    After the sign-in, your device is added to your account and a message suggests restarting. Closing Word and opening it again is enough. You do not need to reboot the whole machine.

  10. 10

    Confirm it worked

    Your initials (or your picture) should show in the top right corner of Word, and Blank document should open a new document. That is the whole thing — Excel and PowerPoint are already signed in too.

    You're done

Since this was recorded

Microsoft rearranges this site regularly, so a button may be worded or placed a little differently than in the video. Two things stay true and are what you should steer by: enter through Microsoft 365 for free, and the installer lives under All apps → Install apps. If the page in front of you looks nothing like the video, message me through the Canvas Inbox rather than paying for anything.

What gets installed

The installer puts the whole suite on at once. You only need three of them for this course; the rest are yours to keep or remove.

Apps included in the Microsoft 365 desktop install
AppNeeded for CSC 104?Notes
WordYesUsed throughout, and the Week 6 Word Project is built in it.
ExcelYesUsed throughout, and the Week 11 Excel Project is built in it.
PowerPointYesUsed in the presentation chapters.
OutlookNoInstalls as “Outlook classic.” Fine to keep, not used in class.
OneNoteNoNote-taking. Handy, but nothing in this course requires it.
PublisherNoWindows only.
AccessNoWindows only.
SkypeNoNot used in class.

Don't want the extras? Leave them until the course is over, then remove them one at a time from Settings → Apps on Windows, or by dragging them out of the Applications folder on a Mac. Removing individual apps does not affect Word, Excel or PowerPoint.

About Copilot

The college license most likely does not include the Copilot desktop apps. You are welcome to try, but nothing in this course needs them — this is a productivity applications class, not an AI class. Remember too that the course AI policy is limited: use AI to explain, translate or troubleshoot, but build every file you submit yourself.

If something goes wrong

The problems that actually come up, and what to do
What you're seeingWhat to do
It's asking me to pay, or offering 50% off You entered through the student-offer page. Back out and start again from Microsoft 365 for free. You have not been charged.
“We couldn't find an account with that email” Use your full college email address. A personal Gmail, Outlook or Hotmail address will not work here.
I signed in and landed on Copilot That's expected. Go to Apps and more → All apps → Install apps.
I can't find the Install apps button It's in the upper right of the All apps page, not in the main list. Jump to 4:25 in the video.
Word is asking me to sign in again after installing Normal, and only once. Use the same college address. It's linking your copy to the college license.
It says I need to restart my computer Close Word and open it again. That's enough — you don't need to reboot.
Office was already on this computer under a personal account Open Word, go to File → Account, sign out of the personal account, and sign in with your college address. If that gets messy, message me before uninstalling anything.
I don't have a computer I can install on Message me through the Canvas Inbox this week and we'll arrange Splashtop access to a campus lab computer.
Something else entirely Message me through the Canvas Inbox. Say which step you're on and what the screen says — a screenshot helps. Don't pay for anything.
