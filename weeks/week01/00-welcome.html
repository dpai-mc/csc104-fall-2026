What to do this week

This is everything asked for in the course introduction video, in the order it makes sense to do it. Only one item is graded — but the ungraded ones are what make Week 2 possible, so don't leave them. Each time stamp jumps the video below to the part that explains it.

The theme of the whole video is "don't wait." Week 1 is short — the section starts Tuesday 9/1 and the week closes Sunday 9/6. Getting the book and Office sorted out now is the difference between a normal Week 2 and a bad one.

  1. 1

    Watch the course introduction video and read through the Start Here page

    The video is right below this list. When you finish Week 1, collapse the Week 1 module in Canvas so Week 2 is easy to find next time.

    Not graded

  2. 2

    Get the textbook and your MindTap access

    The Shelly Cashman Series Collection, Microsoft 365 & Office. It is digital only — there is no paper copy — and it comes as a rental. MindTap access is required, and nearly every graded assignment after this week runs through it.

    Check first: if you already have Cengage Unlimited for another course, this book and the MindTap access are included. Log in and enter your Cengage Unlimited information rather than buying again.

    Not graded Do it now, not in Week 3

  3. 3

    Install the desktop version of Microsoft 365

    It is free for all Mendocino College students. A step-by-step installation video is being posted later this week — watch for the Canvas announcement.

    The web and phone versions are not enough. They are missing features this course asks you to use, so install the desktop version on a Windows or Mac computer.

    Not graded

  4. 4

    No Windows or Mac computer? Message me this week

    If you're on a Chromebook, or on any machine you can't install software on, send me a message through the Canvas Inbox as soon as possible. We'll set you up with Splashtop so you can remote into the campus lab computers, which already have Office installed.

    It adds a few steps — log in, remote in, copy your files up, copy them back down, log out — but it works. What doesn't work is discovering the problem in Week 3.

    Not graded Only if this is you

  5. 5

    Read through the syllabus

    It's under Course Information. It's long, but you only need to read it once. If you want your own copy, use Download Syllabus and save the PDF.

    Not graded

  6. 6

    New to MindTap? Do the orientation material

    There's a reading and a video on the Start Here page covering how to turn assignments in and how to check your grade. Either one is fine — you don't need both. If you've used MindTap before, skip this entirely.

    Not graded

  7. 7

    Shaky on Canvas? Take the free self-enroll course

    Linked from the policies section. Optional, but worth an hour if Canvas itself is the part you're worried about.

    Optional

  8. 8

    Post your introduction to the Introductions discussion

    This is the graded assignment for Week 1. The assignment page gives you the questions to answer and a form that builds your post for you — fill in the boxes, select Build my post, then copy it and paste it into the Canvas discussion.

    Graded — 15 of 25 pts Due any time Friday 9/4

  9. 9

    Reply to two classmates' posts

    Two different people. Be specific about why you're replying to that post — "hi, nice to meet you, good luck" is not a reply. Show the other student you actually read what they wrote.

    Look for posts nobody has replied to yet, especially if you're doing this Sunday night. Everybody should get a response. And the earlier you post your own introduction, the more replies you'll get back.

    Graded — 10 of 25 pts Due Sunday 9/6, 11:59 PM

Since this was recorded

A few things in the video are being sorted out or have already changed. Where this page and the video disagree, this page is right.

  • Add, drop, refund and withdrawal dates. The video and the syllabus show the full-term dates. This is a 15-week late-start section, so your deadlines may differ. They are being confirmed with Admissions and Records and will be posted as soon as they are settled — check your Class Schedule in the meantime. (Video 8:11 and 17:34.)
  • Student hours. The syllabus lists all student hours online via Zoom. In the recording I mention that the Tuesday times may be in my office — go by the syllabus, and if you want to meet in person, make an appointment on my calendar and say so in the notes. (Video 5:11.)
  • The Week 1 assignment. The wrong assignment was briefly linked in the module while I was recording; you can watch me notice it and fix it. The correct assignment is the Introductions discussion. (Video 19:02.)
  • Discussion due dates. The syllabus lists a single Sunday 9/6 deadline for this discussion. The dates that count are the ones in the video and on this page: your post is due Friday 9/4 and your two replies are due Sunday 9/6, so there is something on the board to reply to.
