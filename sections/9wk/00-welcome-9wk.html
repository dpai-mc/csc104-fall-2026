How the nine weeks work

This section covers the same course as the 15-week section — the same book, the same assignments, the same outcomes — in nine weeks. That means each week covers two topics instead of one, and the syllabus is honest about the cost: plan on about 17 hours a week.

Part A · Monday–Wednesday

One topic. Due Wednesday at 11:59 PM.

Part B · Thursday–Sunday

The next topic. Due Sunday at 11:59 PM.

Two exceptions: Thanksgiving week (Week 7) has only the Monday–Wednesday part, and the last week's second part runs Thursday–Friday and is due Friday 12/11, when everything in the course closes.

Every Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook part has the same pair of MindTap assignments: a guided assignment (25 pts) and a homework assignment (100 pts). All deadlines are 11:59 PM.

Late work is shorter here

MindTap work and the File Management assignment are accepted late with a 15% deduction only until the next deadline — Wednesday work until that Sunday, Sunday work until the following Wednesday — and then they close. The Word and Excel Projects are not accepted late unless you arrange it with me before the due date. Your lowest 25-point score and lowest 100-point score are dropped automatically, but in nine weeks that safety net goes fast.

So rather than re-record everything, each reused page in Canvas has a note box at the top, above the page itself. It looks like this:

When the box and the page disagree, the box wins. If a video says "this is due Sunday" or "next week we start Excel," check the box above it. If there's no box and something still looks off, go by your 9-week syllabus and message me.

How a guided-assignment page works

Once Word starts, each part's guided assignment comes with a page like this one: a video where I walk through the entire assignment from start to finish, a searchable transcript, and a detailed list of key moments. The point of the video is to show you where things are in the menus and how I actually use the applications — including shortcuts the book doesn't mention.

  • Stuck on one step? Find it in the key moments and jump straight there, rather than re-watching from the top.
  • Want a bigger picture? The videos are on YouTube, so you can pop one out into its own window from the player.
  • Then do the homework assignment on your own. It uses the same commands on a different document.

Since this was recorded

A few things in the video need a correction or a word of explanation. Where this page and the video disagree, this page is right.

  • The second part of each week runs Thursday–Sunday. In the video I say "Thursday to Saturday." Thursday–Sunday work is due Sunday at 11:59 PM. The only short second part is Week 9, which runs Thursday–Friday and is due Friday 12/11. (Video 0:33.)
  • Add, drop and withdrawal dates. The dates in the syllabus's policies section are full-term dates and do not apply to this section; a 9-week section has its own, earlier deadlines. Check your Class Schedule or ask Enrollment Services this week. (Video 16:59.)
  • "The introductory video will be different." This is that video — this page is your course introduction. The installation video on the Install Microsoft Office page applies to you exactly as shown. (Video 7:54.)
  • Temporary book access. I mention that you can get into the book free for the first week. Don't plan around it — Word 1 is due Wednesday 10/21, so have the book and MindTap in place before Week 2 starts on Monday 10/19. (Video 11:45.)
  • Two gaps in the transcript. The transcript skips 7:23–7:54 and 10:46–11:09, where the automatic captioning produced nothing usable, and there's a quiet pause around 12:15 while I sort out the Student View mix-up. The video itself plays straight through; nothing important is missing from this page.
